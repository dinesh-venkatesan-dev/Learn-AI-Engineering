# Sustainability report 2025

## Packaging
In 2025, 40% of cafés switched to compostable cups. Retail bean bags are now 100% recyclable.

## Energy
The Bengaluru roastery ran on 35% solar power in 2025.

## Sourcing
All beans are bought directly from farms, and every partner farm is visited at least once a year.

## Waste
Coffee grounds from all Bengaluru cafés are collected and given to local farms as compost.
