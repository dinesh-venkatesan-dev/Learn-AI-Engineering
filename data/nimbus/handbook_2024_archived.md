# Nimbus Coffee Co. — Employee Handbook (2024 edition, archived)

> This edition was replaced on 1 January 2025. Kept for reference only.

## Working hours
Cafés open at 7:30 and close at 20:00. Baristas work 8-hour shifts with a 30-minute paid break.

## Leave policy
Every employee gets 20 days of paid annual leave per year.
Unused leave can be carried over, up to a maximum of 5 days, into the next year.
Sick leave is 10 days per year.

## Remote work
Head office staff may work remotely on Fridays only.

## Pay and benefits
Salaries are paid on the 25th of every month.
The yearly learning budget is 20,000 rupees per employee.
