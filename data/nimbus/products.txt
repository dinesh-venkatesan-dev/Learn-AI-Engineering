Nimbus Coffee Co. sells three signature bean blends.

Monsoon Malabar Reserve is a low-acidity blend from the Malabar coast of Kerala, with notes of dark chocolate and spice. It is roasted dark and is best for espresso. A 250 g bag costs 650 rupees.

Nilgiri Sunrise is a light roast from the Nilgiri hills with bright citrus and honey notes. It is best for pour-over and filter coffee. A 250 g bag costs 720 rupees.

Chikmagalur Classic is a medium roast grown on estates in Chikmagalur, with caramel and nutty notes. It is the most popular blend and works well with milk. A 250 g bag costs 560 rupees.

All beans are roasted every Monday in the Bengaluru roastery and shipped within 48 hours.
Orders above 1,500 rupees ship for free. Subscriptions give a 15% discount and can be paused anytime.
Unopened bags can be returned within 14 days for a full refund. Opened bags cannot be returned.
