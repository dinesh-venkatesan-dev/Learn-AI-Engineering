# The Nimbus barista academy

## Training
Every new barista completes a 2-week training programme before working a shift alone.
The programme covers espresso, milk texturing, brewing methods and customer service.

## Certification
Baristas can take the advanced certification after 6 months. Certified baristas earn 10% more.

## The academy
Today training happens in the Bengaluru head office kitchen. A dedicated academy will open in Pune.
