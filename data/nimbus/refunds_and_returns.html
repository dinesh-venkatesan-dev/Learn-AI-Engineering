Refunds and returns

Damaged deliveries

If your bag arrives damaged, send a photo to orders@nimbus.example within 3 days and we will send a replacement.

Cancelling an order

Orders can be cancelled free of charge until they are packed. Packed orders must be returned instead.

How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days.

Café purchases

Drinks and food bought in a café are not refundable, but we will happily remake a drink you don't like.
