# IT security policy

## Accounts and passwords
Every account belongs to one person; sharing logins is not allowed.
Use a password manager. Never reuse a work password on another site.
Accounts are locked after 5 failed sign-in attempts and unlocked by the IT desk.

## Devices
Work laptops use full-disk encryption and lock after 5 minutes of inactivity.
Personal phones may access email only through the company's mobile management app.
Report a lost or stolen phone to the IT desk the same day.

## Data
Customer data must never be copied to personal devices or personal cloud storage.
Confidential documents are shared only through the company drive, never as email attachments.

## Incidents
Suspected phishing emails must be forwarded to security@nimbus.example and then deleted.
