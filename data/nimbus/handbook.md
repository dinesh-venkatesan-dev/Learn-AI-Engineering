# Nimbus Coffee Co. — Employee Handbook

## Working hours
Cafés open at 7:00 and close at 21:00. Baristas work 8-hour shifts with a 45-minute paid break.
Head office staff work 9:30 to 18:00, Monday to Friday.

## Leave policy
Every employee gets 24 days of paid annual leave per year.
Unused leave can be carried over, up to a maximum of 8 days, into the next year.
Sick leave is 12 days per year and needs a doctor's note after 2 consecutive days.
Parents get 26 weeks of paid parental leave.

## Remote work
Head office staff may work remotely on Tuesdays and Thursdays.
Café staff cannot work remotely.
Remote work outside India needs written approval from the Head of People, Meera Iyer.

## Pay and benefits
Salaries are paid on the last working day of every month.
Every employee gets a free drink per shift and a 40% discount on beans.
The yearly learning budget is 30,000 rupees per employee, for courses and books.
Health insurance covers the employee, their spouse and up to two children.

## Travel and expenses
Domestic flights must be economy class. Hotels are reimbursed up to 6,000 rupees per night.
Meals during travel are reimbursed up to 1,500 rupees per day.
Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days with receipts attached.

## Equipment and security
Head office staff receive a laptop on day one. Laptops are replaced every 3 years.
Lost laptops must be reported to IT within 24 hours at it-help@nimbus.example.
Passwords must be at least 14 characters and are rotated every 180 days.
Two-factor authentication is mandatory for email and the payroll system.

## Code of conduct
Employees must treat customers and colleagues with respect.
Gifts from suppliers worth more than 2,000 rupees must be declared to the compliance team.
