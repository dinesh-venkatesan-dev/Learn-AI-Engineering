# Franchise FAQ

## Can I open a Nimbus café?
Nimbus works with franchise partners for cafés outside India. Within India, all cafés are company-owned.

## What does a franchise partner provide?
The partner finds the site, funds the fit-out and hires local staff. Nimbus supplies beans, training and the brand.

## How long does it take to open?
A new franchise café usually takes 9 to 12 months from signing to opening.

## What does it cost?
The franchise fee is agreed per market. Partners also pay a royalty of 6% of café revenue.
