Orders and delivery

How long does delivery take?

Metro cities receive orders in 2 business days. Other locations take 4 to 6 business days.

Can I change my delivery address?

Yes, until the order is packed. Write to orders@nimbus.example with your order ID.

Loyalty programme

How do Nimbus Beans points work?

You earn 1 point for every 10 rupees spent. 500 points can be redeemed for a free 250 g bag.

Do points expire?

Points expire 12 months after they are earned.

Cafés

Do you have Wi-Fi?

All cafés have free Wi-Fi. Sessions are limited to 2 hours.
