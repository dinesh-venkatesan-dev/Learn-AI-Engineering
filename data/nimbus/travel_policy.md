# Travel and expenses policy (detailed)

## Booking
All travel is booked through the travel desk at least 7 days in advance.
International travel needs approval from a director.

## Flights and trains
Trains are preferred for trips under 6 hours. Business class is allowed only on flights longer than 8 hours.

## Hotels by city
Mumbai and Delhi hotels are booked from the approved list. Other cities follow the standard nightly limit in the handbook.
Staying with friends or family earns a 1,000 rupee daily allowance instead of a hotel.

## Per diem abroad
Meals abroad are reimbursed up to 60 US dollars per day.

## Claims
Claims are approved by your manager and paid with the next salary.
Taxi receipts are required for any ride above 500 rupees.
